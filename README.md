# Churn Prediction and Customer Retention intelligence
### Customer churn, the loss of customers who stop using a service, reduces revenue and is usually noticed only after it has happened. This project builds a web-based system that scores how likely a customer is to leave, explains the score and suggests a next step for the retention team. Using records of 14,606 electricity and gas customers and their monthly prices, three classifiers (Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost) were trained and compared. XGBoost gave the best F1 score (0.296) and a ROC-AUC of 0.689 on held-out data; customers it scores in the High band churned at 22.2% against 9.7% overall, and that band captures 44% of all churners. For each customer the system shows a 0 to 100 risk score, the inputs that raised or lowered it, and one of four rule-based retention actions with a plain-English reason. The system is built with FastAPI, React and MongoDB.
